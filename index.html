About Doctor

Dr. Ashutosh Malviya

Dr. Ashutosh Malviya is a highly experienced Ayurvedic doctor with more than 43 years of experience in Ayurvedic medicine. He provides natural and effective treatment using traditional Ayurvedic methods.

He is a specialist in the treatment of Pneumonia, Liver problems, and various stomach-related diseases. His treatments focus on improving overall health and treating the root cause of diseases.

Ayurveda has been a proud tradition in his family for generations. For the past 8 generations, his family has been serving people through Ayurvedic medicine. Dr. Ashutosh Malviya continues this legacy as the 8th generation Ayurvedic doctor in his family.
